Model

  • Gas: piecewise power-law Q = 1 solutions of Clarke (2009), Table A1, rescaled with M★1/3. β = 4 / (9 αGI).
  • Regimes: gas fragmentation αGI > 0.06; no GI αGI < 10⁻⁴; MRI where αGI < 0.01 and (T > 1000 K or r > 100 au).
  • Dust: Stmax = β (vfrag/cs)² (eq. 6); ε(Stmax) from eq. 7; cd/cs from the implicit eq. (C1), with Λ evaluated at the temperature ratio (cd/cs)², if λJ > ΔrSt, else (St/β)1/2. Where eq. (C1) has several roots (St ~ 0.2–1) the largest is taken: it is the stable branch reached by grains growing from small St. DDGI where cd/cs < ε (dashed line).
  • Λ from the two-fluid marginal stability curve, precomputed on a (b, ε, St) grid and interpolated in log space.
  • MJ = λJ,g² ΛJ² Σg, with λJ,g = cs²/(πGΣg) and ΛJ = Λ(ε, (cd/cs)², St → ∞)/(1+ε). tgrowth from eq. 14 with St0 = πρsa0/(2Σg); tDDGI = Ω⁻¹ [ΛJ/(3(1+ε))]1/2.
